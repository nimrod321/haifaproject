Welcome to
Dilemma Street!

In this game, you and another player will each make a secret choice.

Your points depend on BOTH of your choices.

Let's learn how to play!

The Game Board

⬆️ OTHER PLAYER's choices are at the top (columns A & B)

3 | 3
0 | 5
5 | 0
1 | 1

⬅️ YOUR choices are on the left (rows A & B)

The orange number is YOUR score. The purple number is the other player's.

How to Play

1️⃣

Tap Row A or Row B to pick your choice

2️⃣

The wooden doors will slide open to show your possible scores

3️⃣

Press the SEND button to lock in your choice

4️⃣

Wait for the other player, then see the result!

Scoring

🏴☠️

Your points fly into the treasure chest at the bottom of the screen.

Play all the rounds to finish the game.

Your total score appears on the leaderboard!
